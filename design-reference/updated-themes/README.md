The images are complete screen concepts, including illustrative user data and
some device frames. Recreate their backgrounds as native gradients and abstract
shapes, with semantic theme tokens for glass surfaces, fine edges, text and
controls. Do not use a complete screenshot as the application's wallpaper.
Light and dark are two variants of each family, not different navigation trees.
Respect Reduce Transparency/Reduce Motion and readable foreground contrast.

`foodlogpage.png`, `workoutpage.png` and `supplementpage.png` are additional page
references for the later screen phases. Food shows a summary, prominent
Add Food/Create Meal/Scan or Track/Favorites actions, and a meal timeline.
Workout shows a muscle figure, editing/planning/history actions, today's
scheduled workout, progress and exercises. Supplements shows adherence, add/
stack/dose/reminder actions, stack cards and schedule. Their example records,
inventory counts, pictures and reminders are not live data or schema authority.

Phase Two applies this visual language to authentication, profile completion
and reusable loading/failure surfaces. Home and feature screens follow their
approved screen-phase work. Where these examples vary navigation or move page
sections, reconcile that explicitly in the corresponding screen plan.
